# Dataset Provenance

## Training Data

The DevMate training dataset consists of conversational
question-and-answer examples for programming assistance.

The original examples were AI-generated, according to the
project author's recollection. The original generation tool,
prompts, and applicable output terms have not yet been verified.

Additional examples were added during later fine-tuning
iterations. See the training scripts and experiment reports
for details.

## Licensing and Limitations

The dataset's complete provenance and licensing status remain
unverified. AI-generated content is not automatically free of
third-party rights or usage restrictions.

Do not treat this dataset as independently verified or claim
that all examples are exclusively human-authored.

Review the relevant AI tool terms and any identifiable
third-party source material before redistributing the dataset.
